Our story

2025 didn’t go to plan.

Aberavon Beach Fest 2025 was called off at the last minute over safety arrangements. So many of you had made plans, and we were as gutted as you were.

We haven’t given up on it. For 2027 we’re starting early, working with everyone involved from day one, and building it together with the community, so the whole town gets the weekend it deserves.

  1. You say yesEvery name on the list shows there’s real demand for a festival on our beach.
  2. We plan it properlyPermissions, safety and partners, sorted early and done right.
  3. June 2027A family fun weekend on the greatest beach in the world.

What’s in store

A family weekend on the beach.

Here’s what we’d love to bring back to the seafront, with something for every age. Tell us what you’d come for when you sign up.

Live music

Bands and artists on the seafront, from local favourites to big-name tributes. Bring your voice.

Little ones

Kids Fest

A mini festival for the little ones: performers, meet-and-greets, face painting, glitter and games.

Steel Town Market

Local crafts, small businesses and delicious street food from across the town.

Holistic mornings

Start the day with beach yoga, sound healing, a cold plunge and a sauna by the sea.

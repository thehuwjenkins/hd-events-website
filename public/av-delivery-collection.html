One trip. Delivered, crewed and collected.

Most delivery services drop the kit at the door and leave. Ours are delivered by working AV technicians, so the person who arrives with your equipment knows exactly what it is, where it goes and how to make it work. They can unload, set up and test, or simply hand over to your team.

If you need an extra pair of hands, they can stay on as crew for the event. When the night is over, they pack down and load out there and then. No separate collection booking, no van coming back the next morning, and no tired crew trying to find someone to collect after a late finish.

We are a lean business without a central London warehouse, so we do not carry central London overheads. That keeps our rates competitive, while our technicians cover London and the rest of the UK.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

How is this different from a normal courier?

Your equipment is delivered by experienced AV technicians rather than general drivers. They can set up and test on arrival, stay on as crew, and pack down and collect at the end, so one booking covers everything.

Can the driver stay on and help during the event?

Yes. Our technician can stay on as crew during the event and then collect everything at the end of the night. This is charged as additional crew time.

Can you collect at the end of the night instead of the next morning?

Yes. Same-night collection is one of the main benefits. It saves a second trip, avoids next-morning venue access problems and means your own crew can go home after a late finish.

Do you only deliver your own equipment?

No. We can deliver and collect equipment from other hire companies, venues or production stores, as well as our own.

Do you cover London?

Yes. We cover London and the rest of the UK. Because we do not run a central London warehouse, our rates are competitive.

How much does AV delivery and collection cost?

Delivery starts from £85 each way. The final price depends on distance, the size of the load and how long you need the technician on site. Send us the pick-up and delivery addresses, times and what is being moved, and we will send a clear quote.

Can you do several pick-ups or drop-offs in one trip?

Yes. Multi-drop runs let us collect from or deliver to several locations in a single trip for a small additional charge, which costs far less than booking separate deliveries.

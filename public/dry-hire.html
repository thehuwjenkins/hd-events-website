FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

What is dry hire?

Dry hire means hiring the equipment only, without our technicians. It suits production companies, venues and bands who have their own crew.

What brands do you hire?

KV2 Audio, NEXO, Allen & Heath, Yamaha, Sennheiser and sE Electronics.

Can you deliver the equipment?

Yes, delivery and collection can be arranged, or you can collect.

Can I add a technician to a dry hire?

Yes. If you need help on the day we can add a technician for setup or the full event.

How do I check availability?

Send us your equipment list, venue and dates using the quote form or WhatsApp, and we will confirm availability and price.

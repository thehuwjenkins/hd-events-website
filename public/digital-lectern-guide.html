What is a digital lectern?

A digital lectern is a speaker's lectern with a screen built into the front, and often a second screen on top for the speaker. The front screen shows your logo, sponsor slides, speaker names or live video to the audience and cameras. The top screen, called a comfort monitor, lets the speaker see their slides, notes or a timer without turning round.

Because the lectern appears in almost every photo and livestream of an event, the front screen is one of the most valuable pieces of branding in the room. Unlike a printed panel, it can change between speakers in seconds.

A well-built digital lectern also tidies up the stage. Power, video and microphone connections come together at one rear panel, and with network-cable video extenders such as Kramer CAT6, a single cable can carry the picture from the control desk.

What to look for in a digital lectern

Front screen

Size and brightness matter most. It should be bright enough for a lit stage and look sharp on camera. Portrait screens suit logos and speaker names.

Comfort monitor

A screen on the top surface for the speaker's slides, notes or a countdown. It makes speakers more confident and keeps them facing the audience.

Microphone

A gooseneck microphone suits most speeches. Make sure it connects cleanly to the venue's sound system and that a wireless mic can be used alongside it.

Connections

Look for a single rear panel with clearly labelled power, video and audio inputs. CAT6 video extenders let you run one long network cable instead of fragile HDMI.

Build & transport

A lectern that travels needs a strong frame, protected screens and a flight case. One that stays in a venue can focus on finish and cable management.

Support

Check who builds it, who installs it and who to call if something stops working. Buying from the builder makes repairs and upgrades simpler.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

What is a digital lectern?

A digital lectern is a speaker's lectern with a built-in screen on the front, and often a comfort monitor on top. The front screen shows branding, sponsor slides, speaker names or live video, and the top screen shows the speaker their slides or notes.

How much does a digital lectern cost in the UK?

HD Events custom digital lecterns are built to order from £1,465 excluding VAT. The final price depends on the screens, microphone and connection options. Hiring one costs from £250 per day plus delivery.

Should I buy or hire a digital lectern?

Buy if your venue hosts speakers regularly, such as a hotel, conference centre, university or council chamber. Hire if you need one for a single event or a few events a year.

What is a comfort monitor on a lectern?

A comfort monitor is a screen on the top of the lectern, facing the speaker. It shows their slides, notes, a timer or the next slide, so they can present without turning to look at the main screen.

How does a digital lectern connect to the AV system?

Usually through a rear panel with power, video and microphone connections. CAT6 video extenders such as Kramer let one network cable carry the picture from the control desk, keeping the stage tidy.

Who makes custom digital lecterns in the UK?

HD Events designs and builds custom digital lecterns in the UK, including a matching pair for the Brangwyn Hall in Swansea, and delivers and installs them across the UK, including London.

Can a digital lectern be branded?

Yes. The front screen can show your logo, colours, sponsors or event artwork, and change between speakers. The body and finish can also be specified when the lectern is built.

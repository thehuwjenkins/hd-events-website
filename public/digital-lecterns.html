A lectern that works as hard as your speaker.

A digital lectern turns the most-photographed spot in the room into a screen for your brand. Show your logo between speakers, sponsor content, speaker names or live slides, and change it in seconds.

Every lectern is built to order around how it will be used: the screen, the microphone, the speaker's monitor and how it connects to your AV system. We recently built a matching pair for the Brangwyn Hall in Swansea.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

How much does a digital lectern cost?

Our custom digital lecterns start from £1,465. The final price depends on the screen, comfort monitor, microphone and connectivity options you choose.

Can I hire a digital lectern?

Yes. Our digital lecterns are available to hire from £250 per day plus delivery, either on their own or as part of a full AV package.

What can be shown on the lectern screen?

Anything you can send to a display: your logo, sponsor slides, speaker names, video or a live feed of the presentation.

How does the lectern connect to the AV system?

We can fit Kramer CAT6 converters so video and signals travel over a single network cable, keeping the stage tidy and the connection reliable.

Do you deliver and install lecterns across the UK?

Yes. We deliver and set up lecterns across the UK, including London.

How long does a custom build take?

Lead times depend on the specification. We will confirm the timescale when we quote.

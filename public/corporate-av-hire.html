FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

What AV do I need for a conference?

Most conferences need a sound system sized to the room, wireless microphones for each presenter and the panel, a presentation screen with a way to play slides and video, and lighting on the stage or lectern. Rooms over around 150 guests usually benefit from relay screens so everyone has a clear view. Send us your venue and running order and we will specify exactly what is needed.

Do you provide a technician on the day?

Yes. For conference and AGM packages a technician from our own team operates sound, slides and lighting throughout the event. For smaller briefings we can set up, test and hand over, or stay on site, whichever you prefer.

Do you cover events outside London?

Yes. We focus on London but deliver corporate events across the UK, including Wales, the South West, the Midlands and the North.

How much does corporate AV hire cost?

Our Speaker Essentials package for smaller rooms starts from £500. Larger conferences and multi-room events are quoted individually, as the cost depends on audience size, screens, lighting and the length of the day. Every quote is itemised.

Can you work with our venue's in-house AV?

Yes. We regularly work alongside venue teams, either providing the full technical package or adding to what the venue already has, such as extra microphones, lecterns or relay screens.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as possible for busy conference periods, typically spring and autumn. We can often help at shorter notice, so it is always worth asking.

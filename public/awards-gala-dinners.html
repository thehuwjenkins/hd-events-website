One technical team, from first course to final song.

An awards night moves quickly between very different moments: speeches over dinner, a host on stage, winners walking up to music, sponsor videos, and a band or DJ to finish. We design one system that handles all of it, so the evening flows without changeovers or surprises.

We have produced dinners in hotel ballrooms, historic halls and unusual spaces, including a corporate dinner for several hundred guests beneath Concorde in its hangar.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

What AV does an awards ceremony need?

Typically a sound system for speech and music, a lectern and presenter microphones, screens for category titles and nominee content, walk-up music for each winner, and stage lighting so winners look good in photos. We put it all on one cue sheet so the night runs smoothly.

Can you play our nominee videos and sponsor slides?

Yes. Send us the content in advance and we build it into the show, then operate it live from the control desk.

Can you also provide sound for a band or DJ after dinner?

Yes. We design the system for both speeches and music, so there is no changeover between the formal part of the evening and the entertainment.

Do you work outside London?

Yes. We deliver awards and gala dinners across the UK.

How much does awards night AV cost?

Every awards night is different, so we quote individually based on guest numbers, screens, lighting and the running order. Send us your brief and we will send an itemised quote.

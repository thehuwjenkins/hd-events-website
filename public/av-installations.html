Technology your team can actually use.

The best installation is one that works every time someone walks in and switches it on. We start with a site survey: how the room sounds, how it is used, who will operate the system and what has gone wrong before. From that we design a bespoke specification, not an off-the-shelf package.

Our background is running live events, so we know what works in practice: speech reinforcement that stays clear in reverberant halls and churches, presentation connections that work first time, and lighting presets anyone can recall at the touch of a button.

We install neatly and with as little disruption as possible, working around your services, lessons or events. At handover we train your team, leave simple written guides, and stay on hand for support, servicing and future upgrades.

SERVICES

Systems we design and install

Fixed installation sound systems

Speech and music sound systems designed for the room's size and acoustics, from discreet column speakers to full performance PA.

Speech reinforcement

Clear, natural speech for halls, churches, council chambers and lecture rooms, so every word reaches the back row without feedback.

Microphone packages

Lectern, gooseneck, wireless handheld, headset and lapel microphones, sized to how many people speak or perform at once.

Audio control & processing

Digital mixing, DSP and zone control with simple wall panels or presets, so volunteers and staff get the right sound every time.

Integrated lighting systems

Stage, house and architectural LED lighting with DMX control and recallable scenes for services, shows and presentations.

Display & projection systems

Screens, projectors and confidence monitors, with tidy HDMI or CAT6 connection points where presenters need them.

Digital lecterns

Our own custom-built digital lecterns, designed to match your venue and wired into the installed system.

Upgrades & integration

We assess what you already have, reuse what is worth keeping and integrate new equipment with it, rather than starting again.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

What does an AV installation include?

A site survey, a bespoke system design, supply of the equipment, installation and commissioning, and training for your team at handover. We can also provide ongoing support and servicing.

How much does an AV installation cost?

Every installation is designed for the space, so we quote after a site survey. The cost depends on the size of the room, the number of microphones and speakers, whether screens and lighting are included and how much cabling is needed. Quotes are itemised, and we can phase the work to suit your budget.

Do you install sound systems in churches and halls?

Yes. We design and install sound systems for churches, halls and other venues, with careful attention to speech clarity in reverberant spaces.

Can you upgrade our existing system?

Yes. We assess what you have, recommend upgrades and reuse equipment where it makes sense, so you are not paying to replace things that still work well.

Will our staff or volunteers be able to use it?

Yes. We design the controls around the people who will use them, with simple presets or wall panels where possible, and train your team at handover.

Do you install integrated lighting and screens as well as sound?

Yes. We design sound, lighting and display systems together so they work as one, including digital lecterns built by us.

Do you provide support after installation?

Yes. We provide support after handover and can arrange regular servicing to keep the system in good working order.

Production that serves the performance.

Great live sound is about more than volume. It is about clarity, balance and a system that suits the room, whether that is a cathedral, a theatre or a stadium. We specify and engineer every show to sound natural for the audience and comfortable for the performers.

We also provide production and stage management for theatre and touring work, coordinating venues, schedules and technical requirements so the performance can take centre stage.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Do you provide sound engineers for concerts?

Yes. Our engineers mix front of house and monitors, or work alongside an artist's own engineer, using our Allen & Heath and Yamaha consoles.

Can you provide sound for churches and cathedrals?

Yes. Historic buildings need careful system design because of their long reverberation. We regularly provide sound for concerts and services in churches and cathedrals.

Do you provide theatre production and stage management?

Yes. We provide production, tour and stage management alongside sound and lighting.

Can I just hire the equipment?

Yes. If you have your own crew, see our dry hire page for sound and lighting equipment hire.

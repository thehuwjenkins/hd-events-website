London events, without London overheads.

HD Events supplies and runs audio visual equipment for conferences, AGMs, awards dinners, product launches, parties and live music across London. The same team plans the kit, delivers it, sets it up and stays to run it, so there is one point of contact from quote to pack-down.

We do not run a central London warehouse, so we do not pass central London overheads on to you. Our technicians travel in with the equipment for each job, which keeps our rates competitive for London venues of every size.

London venues come with their own rules: booked loading bays, goods lifts, tight access windows and late finishes. We plan the delivery and the load-out around them, and can collect the same night so nobody has to come back in the morning.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Do you supply AV hire in London?

Yes. We supply sound, microphones, screens, lighting and digital lecterns for events across London, delivered, set up and run by our own technicians.

How much does AV hire cost in London?

Our Speaker Essentials package starts from £500 and covers a lectern with microphone, two Sennheiser wireless microphones, a compact PA, a laptop audio connection, and delivery, setup and collection for up to 150 guests. Larger events are quoted individually with every item listed. Prices exclude VAT.

Can you deliver AV equipment to a London venue and collect it the same night?

Yes. Delivery and collection starts from £85 each way. Our drivers are AV technicians, so they can set up, stay on as crew and pack down at the end of the night.

Can I hire a digital lectern in London?

Yes. Our custom digital lecterns can be hired from £250 per day plus delivery, on their own or as part of a full AV package.

Do you work with London venues' in-house AV teams?

Yes. We can provide the full technical package or add to what the venue already has, such as extra microphones, a digital lectern or relay screens.

Why are your London prices competitive?

We do not run a central London warehouse, so we do not carry central London overheads. Our technicians bring the equipment in for each job.

How do I book AV for a London event?

Send us the date, venue, number of guests and what is happening on the day, by the enquiry form, WhatsApp or phone. We reply with a recommended setup and an itemised quote.

QUICK ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

What does HD Events do?

Three things. We hire out sound, microphones, screens and lighting for events, set up and run by our own technicians. We deliver and collect AV equipment, ours or another company’s, with technicians who can set it up and stay on as crew. And we design and build custom digital lecterns for venues to buy, or hire for a single event.

Where do you work?

London and the rest of the UK.

How do I book?

For event AV, use Request a quote. For a delivery, use Book a driver. For a lectern, build and price it on the digital lecterns page. Or WhatsApp us on +44 7930 547024. We reply with a clear quote, and nothing is booked until you accept it.

How much does it cost?

Event AV packages start from £500. AV delivery starts from £85 each way. Custom digital lecterns start from £1,465 to buy, or £250 per day to hire. Lectern prices exclude VAT.

Can you just deliver equipment, without the full event service?

Yes. Book a driver on its own. Our technician delivers and collects, and can set up or stay on as crew if you add it.

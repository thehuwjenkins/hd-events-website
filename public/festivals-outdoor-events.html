FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Can you provide the full stage production for a festival?

Yes. We can provide sound, lighting, screens, stage management and crew, and support the planning, licensing and safety side too.

Do you help with event licensing and risk assessments?

Yes. See our event planning and licensing page. We help coordinate licensing, risk assessments and compliance.

How do you manage noise at outdoor events?

We design and aim the system for even coverage of the audience area, which keeps sound clear for the crowd while reducing spill to neighbours, and we can monitor levels during the event.

Do you cover events across the UK?

Yes. We deliver outdoor events across the UK.
